# 相册日报 隐私政策 · Album Digest Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date：2026-11-06

[简体中文](#简体中文) · [English](#english)

## 简体中文

相册日报（下称“本 App”）是一款在 iPhone 本地运行的相册日报工具。本 App 的设计原则是：你的照片，以及由照片分析出来的任何信息，都不会离开你的设备。

### 我们不收集任何数据

- 本 App 没有账号，不需要登录。
- 本 App 不连接任何服务器，不上传、不出售、不分享任何数据。
- 本 App 不含广告、统计分析或任何第三方 SDK，不追踪你。

### 本 App 在你的设备上处理哪些信息

- **照片图库**：在你授权后，本 App 在设备上读取照片和截图，用 Apple 的 Vision 框架识别场景、文字、人脸数量和美学评分；在支持 Apple 智能的设备上，用 Apple 的端侧大模型生成日报。这些处理全部在你的 iPhone 上完成。
- 原图只保存在 iCloud 时，本 App 只使用设备上已有的缩略图，不会从 iCloud 下载原图。
- 分析结果（场景标签、识别出的文字、日报）保存在本 App 的沙盒里，并标记为不进入 iCloud 和电脑备份。分析结果保留 60 天。
- 可能含有密码、证件号码或银行卡号的截图，其中的文字不会出现在通知里。

### 删除照片

只有你在本 App 里点了删除，本 App 才会请求删除那张照片。系统会先弹窗让你确认；删除的照片会进入“照片”App 的“最近删除”，30 天内可以恢复。

### 通知

本 App 使用本地通知在你设定的时间提醒你，不使用推送服务器。

### 你的选择

- 你可以随时在“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片”里更改或撤销本 App 的照片访问权限，在“设置 → 通知”里关闭通知。
- 删除本 App 会同时删除它保存的全部数据。

### 儿童

本 App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

### 本政策的变更

本政策如有变化，我们会更新本页面和上方的生效日期。

### 联系我们

如有问题，请在 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/kuangre123/xiangce-ribao-support/issues) 提交。

## English

Album Digest ("the app") creates a daily digest of your photo library and runs entirely on your iPhone. Its guiding principle: your photos, and anything derived from them, never leave your device.

### We collect no data

- The app has no accounts and needs no sign-in.
- The app does not connect to any server. It does not upload, sell or share any data.
- The app contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it does not track you.

### What the app processes on your device

- **Photo library**: with your permission, the app reads photos and screenshots on the device and uses Apple's Vision framework to recognize scenes, text, the number of faces and aesthetic scores. On devices with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device foundation model writes the digest. All of this happens on your iPhone.
- When an original is stored only in iCloud, the app uses the thumbnail already on the device and does not download the original.
- Analysis results (scene labels, recognized text, digests) are stored in the app's sandbox and excluded from iCloud and computer backups. Analysis results are kept for 60 days.
- Text from screenshots that may contain passwords, ID numbers or bank card numbers never appears in notifications.

### Deleting photos

The app asks to delete a photo only when you tap delete in the app. The system asks you to confirm first, and deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they can be recovered for 30 days.

### Notifications

The app uses local notifications to remind you at the time you choose. No push server is involved.

### Your choices

- You can change or revoke the app's photo access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos, and turn off notifications in Settings > Notifications.
- Deleting the app deletes all data it stored.

### Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

### Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
